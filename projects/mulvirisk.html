MULVIRISK

The Role of Multiple Infections in Plant Virus Infection Risk

Grant number: PID2021-124671OB-I00
Duration: 01/09/2023 – 31/08/2025 (+6 months extension)
Funder: Ministerio de Ciencia, Innovación/Agencia Estatal de Investigación (MCIN/AEI) — “Una manera de hacer Europa”
PI: Fernando Garcia Arenal
Co-PI: Mark D Wilkinson

MULVIRISK investigates how co-infections influence the emergence of new plant diseases in the context of global environmental change. Combining empirical and computational approaches, it analyzes pathogen interactions and develops simulation models to predict infection risks.

The project also builds a FAIR-compliant database of plant–pathogen interactions, ensuring reusability and integration with broader agricultural data. It bridges ecology, bioinformatics, and FAIR data science to strengthen global food security.
