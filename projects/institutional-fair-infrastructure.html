Institutional FAIR Infrastructure

Ontology-driven data systems for CBGP

Status: Ongoing, internal collaboration
Partners: CBGP Data Management and Administrative Teams

In collaboration with the CBGP Data Management and Administrative Teams, our group is building ontology-driven databases for managing institutional research data, improving interoperability and accessibility.

A knowledge-based design allows all functionalities — data models and user interfaces — to be generated directly from an OWL ontology, enabling adaptable and FAIR-aligned information systems that unify personnel, projects, and publications.
