AMI1 & Growth–Defense Balance

Clarification of the molecular mechanisms connecting the AMI1 knot with the balance between growth and defense

Grant number: BFU2017-82826-R
Duration: 01/01/2018 – 30/09/2021
Funder: Ministerio de Ciencia, Innovación/Agencia Estatal de Investigación (MCIN/AEI)
PI: Stephan Pollman
Co-PI: Mark D Wilkinson

This project examined the molecular mechanisms connecting the AMI1 indole-3-acetamide hydrolase with the balance between plant growth and defense responses, contributing to a deeper understanding of auxin–stress crosstalk in Arabidopsis thaliana.

Related publication: Pérez-Alonso et al. (2020), Journal of Experimental Botany, DOI: 10.1093/jxb/eraa485.
