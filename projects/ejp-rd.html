EJP RD

European Joint Programme on Rare Diseases — Horizon 2020

Grant number: 825575
Duration: 01/02/2019 – 31/07/2024
Funder: European Union’s Horizon 2020 research and innovation programme

EJP RD created a federated ecosystem to connect rare disease resources — registries, biobanks, multi-omics repositories, and knowledge bases — under FAIR principles.

Its Virtual Platform acts as a single access point, allowing federated discovery and query of distributed data while preserving privacy and respecting access conditions. By linking data sources through interoperable standards, EJP RD supports translational research, innovation, and regulatory science across the European rare disease landscape.
